## 💡 Pro Tips

### For Claude AI:
1. **Process chapter by chapter** if your book is long
2. **Review each update** before moving to next chapter
3. **Save as you go** - don't lose your work
4. **Ask Claude to clarify** if something is unclear

### For Images:
1. **Capture priority images first** (top 10)
2. **Use consistent style** (same colors, fonts)
3. **Add annotations** (arrows, boxes, labels)
4. **Test image quality** (zoom in to check readability)

### For Code Snippets:
1. **Copy from IDE** (preserves formatting)
2. **Add comments** (explain key parts)
3. **Keep it readable** (not too long)
4. **Show complete methods** (don't cut off)

1. **Use professional tools** (draw.io is free)
2. **Keep it simple** (don't overcomplicate)
